  language: en
  version: 2017-07-25
  scene_scope: ["global"]
  anchor_scope: ["global"]
  text: |
    Administer once daily, preferably in the morning. Tablets may be divided but must not be chewed or crushed. Swallow with at least half a glass of liquid. Food does not affect bioavailability. Dosage should be individualized to avoid bradycardia.
    Guidance: hypertension 50–100 mg once daily; angina 100–200 mg once daily (may combine with nitrates if needed); arrhythmias 100–200 mg once daily; post-MI maintenance 200 mg once daily; functional disorders with palpitations 100 mg once daily; migraine prophylaxis 100–200 mg once daily.
    Renal impairment: no adjustment needed. Hepatic impairment: generally no change; reduce in severe cases. Elderly: no adjustment. Children 6–18 years studied; <6 years not established.

  domain: medicinal_product_characteristics
  language: en
  version: 2017-07-25
  scene_scope: ["global"]

  text: |
    Contraindicated in: cardiogenic shock; sick sinus syndrome (without pacemaker); second or third-degree AV block; unstable or uncompensated heart failure (pulmonary edema, hypoperfusion, hypotension) or ongoing beta-agonist inotropic therapy; symptomatic bradycardia or hypotension; severe peripheral arterial disease.

  language: en
  version: 2017-07-25
  scene_scope: ["global"]
  anchor_scope: ["global"]
  text: |
    Avoid intravenous verapamil in patients on beta-blockers. May worsen peripheral arterial disease; use with caution in Prinzmetal's angina (prefer cardioselective beta1 agents). Withdrawal must be gradual over ≥2 weeks; maintain low dose ≥4 days before stopping. Before surgery, inform anesthesiologist; avoid high initial doses pre-noncardiac surgery.

  language: en
  version: 2017-07-25

  text: |
    ZOK microsphere system; pH-independent release ~20 h; effect >24 h. Absorption: complete; bioavailability ~50% (first-pass). Protein binding 5–10%. Pediatric 6–17 y: similar PK; clearance rises with weight.
